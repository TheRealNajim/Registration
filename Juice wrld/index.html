The artist

Music without a mask

Jarad Anthony Higgins, known professionally as Juice WRLD, was an American rapper, singer, and songwriter. He emerged from the SoundCloud scene and became one of the defining voices of emo rap, blending melodic hip-hop with the emotional directness of rock and pop-punk.

After beginning as an independent artist in 2015, he signed with Grade A Productions and Interscope Records in 2017. His breakthrough single, Lucid Dreams, carried his deeply personal writing to listeners around the world.

His songs spoke openly about heartbreak, anxiety, addiction, and loneliness. That honesty challenged expectations around masculinity in hip-hop and gave many young listeners language for feelings they struggled to express themselves.

"I love y'all more than life itself."

Juice WRLD, addressing his fans

The legacy

A voice that still carries

Juice WRLD died on December 8, 2019, six days after his 21st birthday. His life was brief, but his influence continues through the music he released, the artists he inspired, and the connection fans still find in his words. By turning private pain into shared experience, he showed how music can make people feel seen.
